## About

I am an assistant professor in the [Department of Mathematical Sciences](https://www.math.snu.ac.kr) at the [Seoul National University (SNU)](https://en.snu.ac.kr). Prior to joining SNU, I was an assistant professor in the [Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering (ISysE)](https://ie.kaist.ac.kr) at the [Korea Advanced Institute of Science and Technology (KAIST)](https://www.kaist.ac.kr/en/), a post-doc in the [Discrete Mathematics Group](https://dimag.ibs.re.kr/) at the Institute for Basic Science (IBS), hosted by [Sang-il Oum](https://dimag.ibs.re.kr/home/sangil/), and a recipient of the [IBS Young Scientist Fellowship (YSF)](https://www.ibs.re.kr/ysf/).

I obtained my Ph.D. in the [Algorithms, Combinatorics, and Optimization (ACO)](http://aco.math.cmu.edu/) program at the [Tepper School of Business, Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/tepper/), advised by [Gérard Cornuéjols](http://integer.tepper.cmu.edu). My thesis won the [Gerald L. Thompson Doctoral Dissertation Award](https://www.cmu.edu/tepper/news/stories/2019/may/commencement-awards-2019.html) for the best Ph.D. thesis in the area of management science, and I was supported by the [Henry J. Gailliot Presidential Fellowship](https://www.cmu.edu/tepper/programs/phd/program/student-awards.html). A part of my thesis received second place in the [INFORMS Optimization Society Best Student Paper Prize Competition](https://www.informs.org/Recognizing-Excellence/Award-Recipients/Dabeen-Lee) 2019.

## Research interests

I am interested in designing algorithms and mathematical programming frameworks for broad areas of optimization spanning discrete, combinatorial, integer, continuous, convex, online, stochastic, robust, and distributionally robust optimization. Currently, I am working on nonlinear combinatorial optimization and the seamless integration of machine learning and optimization with applications in combinatorial decision-making. My latest research projects are on bilevel polynomial optimization, online reinforcement learning theory, stochastic optimization under decision-dependent distributions, first-order methods for constrained optimization, and distributionally robust optimization frameworks.
